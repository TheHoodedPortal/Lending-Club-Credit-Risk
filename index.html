Predictions use this project's fitted models: a Cox survival model for remaining-life probability of default (conditioned on the loan's current age), an LGD model on the amortization drivers evaluated at the projected default time, and outstanding principal as exposure. The portfolio reserve is the loan-level expected credit loss across the active book; the stress slider scales every grade's default rate together.
For demonstration only. Trained on unsecured consumer loans (Lending Club, 2007–2018); no macroeconomic overlay; not a substitute for a lender's own provisioning.
