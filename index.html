Predictions use this project's fitted models: two cause-specific Cox hazards — default and early payoff — walked forward month by month for the remaining-life probability of default (competing risks, conditioned on the loan's current age), a flat ~89% loss-given-default on the outstanding balance (≈11% recovery), and the amortising balance weighted across possible default months as exposure-at-default. The portfolio reserve is the loan-level expected credit loss across the active book, anchored to each loan's actual outstanding balance; the scenario buttons scale every grade's default rate together.
For demonstration only. Trained on unsecured consumer loans (Lending Club, 2007–2018); no macroeconomic overlay; not a substitute for a lender's own provisioning.
